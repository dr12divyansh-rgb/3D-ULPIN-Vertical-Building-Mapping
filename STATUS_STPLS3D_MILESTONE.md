## KEY FINDINGS SO FAR

1. **STPLS3D has severe class imbalance** (16:1 ratio)
   - Class-weighted loss is **essential** (not optional)

2. **CPU training is slow** (~1.1 s/step with batch_size=4)
   - Required `max_train_chunks` subsetting for tractability
   - Full 6416-chunk training would take 23 hours

3. **Preprocessing worked flawlessly**
   - 67 PLY files, 37.2 GB → 7509 chunks in ~2-3 hours
   - Deterministic scene-level splits preserved

4. **Label mapping is reasonable**
   - 19 STPLS3D classes → 6 classes with minimal information loss
   - Building class (24.5% of data) is minority but significant
